# 🏺 Kintsugi: The Art of Precious Scars

An interactive generative art piece exploring the Japanese philosophy of **Kintsugi** (金継ぎ) — the art of repairing broken pottery with gold. This project treats breakage and repair as a beautiful part of an object's history, rather than something to disguise.

## ✨ Features
* **Interactive Repair:** Click or tap on the vessel to "fill" the cracks with flowing gold.
* **Generative Growth:** Every crack follows a unique, organic path, ensuring no two repairs are ever the same.
* **Wabi-sabi Aesthetic:** A minimalist, dark-mode interface designed for focus and reflection.
* **Responsive Design:** Fully playable on desktop and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Built With
* [p5.js](https://p5js.org/) - A JavaScript library for creative coding.
* **HTML5/CSS3** - Custom typography and responsive layouts.
* **GitHub Pages** - For hosting and deployment.

## 📖 Philosophy
> *"The world breaks everyone and afterward many are strong at the broken places."* > — Ernest Hemingway

Kintsugi teaches us that there is beauty in imperfection. By highlighting the cracks, we celebrate the resilience of the object.
